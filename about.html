OUR STORY

From Advertising
Experience to a Broader
Media Network.

Krishna Outdoor has grown with the changing needs of brands and the evolving media landscape.

With a strong foundation in advertising and media, our capabilities today span outdoor, transit, print, electronic, digital and social communication.

Our outdoor and transit network remains at the heart of Krishna Outdoor. At the same time, our wider advertising expertise allows us to support communication requirements that extend beyond a single media format.

We believe brands should not have to think about advertising in isolated channels. A campaign should begin with an objective, an audience and a market — and then explore the media opportunities that can bring that campaign to life.
