OUR STORY

From Advertising
Experience to a Broader
Media Network.

Krishna Outdoor has grown with the changing needs of brands and the evolving media landscape.

With a strong foundation in advertising and media, our capabilities today span outdoor, transit, print, electronic, digital and social communication.

Our outdoor and transit network remains at the heart of Krishna Outdoor. At the same time, our wider advertising expertise allows us to support communication requirements that extend beyond a single media format.

We believe brands should not have to think about advertising in isolated channels. A campaign should begin with an objective, an audience and a market — and then explore the media opportunities that can bring that campaign to life.

Leadership

Rakesh Gandhi

Rakesh Gandhi — Chairman & Managing Director

“In advertising, long-term success is built on trust, relationships and the commitment to deliver.”

Over the years, I have had the privilege of watching the advertising industry evolve in remarkable ways.

Cities have changed. Media has changed. Technology has transformed the way audiences consume information.

Yet one principle has remained constant — brands need to be present where people are.

Our journey has been built around this understanding. For more than four decades, we have worked to develop strong relationships, understand markets and create advertising opportunities that help brands communicate with their audiences.

What I value most about this journey is the trust placed in us by our clients, partners and team.

As we move forward, our focus remains on strengthening our capabilities while continuing to work with the same values that have guided us from the beginning — commitment, relationships and a genuine understanding of our clients' needs.

Leadership

Kevin Gandhi

Kevin Gandhi — Director

“The future of media is not about choosing between traditional and digital. It is about understanding how they can work together.”

Advertising today is moving faster than ever.

Audiences consume media across multiple platforms, while the physical world continues to offer some of the most powerful opportunities for brands to be seen.

For me, the future is about bringing these worlds together.

Our strength is the experience and foundation built over decades. The opportunity ahead is to build on that foundation with new thinking, smarter planning and a deeper understanding of how audiences move through both cities and media platforms.

At Krishna Outdoor, our goal is to continue evolving with the industry while remaining grounded in the values that have earned us the trust of our clients.
